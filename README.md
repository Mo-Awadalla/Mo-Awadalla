## What I’m working on

### KnicksIQ

An AI-powered basketball analytics platform built with FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Redis, React, and vector search. The goal is to make basketball data easier to explore through conversation while grounding answers in real stats and source data.

### MFS-Trader

A quantitative research platform for testing and validating trading strategies. The focus is on reproducible backtests, useful research tools, and rejecting strategies that don’t hold up under scrutiny.

### Automation + ML

At NYC Emergency Management, I’ve worked on internal automation and document-processing systems involving APIs, OCR pipelines, computer vision, background jobs, and data workflows.
